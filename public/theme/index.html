TPA brings together professionals, business leaders, entrepreneurs and the next generation from Tollygunge and beyond. Different disciplines, shared curiosity and a commitment to collective growth.

Start with your TPA account, choose a membership plan and complete your professional details and required evidence. Membership approval follows association review; creating an account does not automatically confer membership.

TPA’s membership categories are Patron, Annual and Life. Confirmed eligibility, fees and benefits will be published by the association.

Published seminars, workshops and discussions appear in Events & Learning. Confirmed dates, speakers and registration details are shown with each published event.

Yes. You can send an inquiry about membership, an event or a potential collaboration without creating an account. Signed-in accounts can also track their own inquiries.

Your private member space keeps your professional profile, membership application, documents and personal event records together. Staff access and member permissions remain separate.
